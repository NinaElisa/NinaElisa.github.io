ABOUT US

Our dream is to live fully in line with our values and create a space that makes the world a better place.

We act from kindness and reflect gratitude.

Nature

We work with nature instead of against it. We only use natural products, organis where possible. We try to aid the ecosystem and are always learning how to care for the nature around us.

The environment

We strive to minimize our footprint and maximize our handprint. We take this into account with every action and decision: food, building, transportation etc.

Community

We want to contribute to the existing community and function as a place where community can be build and felt.

Wellbeing

We believe that connecting with nature and community are at the base of wellbeing. We organize activities that use this as a backdrop to build tools for lasting wellbeing.

What we do

What we do to fulfull our mission

Cultivation

We cultivate plants and trees that nourish the body, the soul and the land. We have avocados, oranges and planning a whole lot, stay tuned for more!

Activities

We organize activities that are in line with our values and enrich the human experience. Find out more on our activities page!
