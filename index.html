ABOUT US


Welcome to Calma el Alma, a small paradise by the Guadalfeo river, near Velez de Benaudalla in Granada province. Here we have the privilege of being surrounded by nature, serving it and facilitating a thriving ecosystem. We aim to decreasing our environmental footprint whilst increasing our handprint and following a cruelty free lifestyle. Our dream is to live fully in line with our values and create a space that makes the world a better place.


We provide a calming and inspiring environment to reconnect with nature, community and the self. We offer workshops focussed on promoting wellbeing and this reconnection. Our goal is to build a community of helpers and givers within which we can support each other and grow together.


Check out our activities and contact us if you want to participate in a workshop, want to give one or have any other ideas!

Nature

We work with nature instead of against it. We only use natural products, organis where possible. We try to aid the ecosystem and are always learning how to care for the nature around us.

The environment

We strive to minimize our footprint and maximize our handprint. We take this into account with every action and decision: food, building, transportation etc.

Community

We want to contribute to the existing community and function as a place where community can be build and felt.

Wellbeing

We believe that connecting with nature and community are at the base of wellbeing. We organize activities that use this as a backdrop to build tools for lasting wellbeing.
